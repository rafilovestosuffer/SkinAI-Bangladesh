    🔬 Model & Condition Insights · রোগ-পরিচিতি

    BD-SkinNet (Swin Transformer Base + CBAM, INT8 quantized) screens 6 dermatological conditions common in Bangladesh:

    Eczema / একজিমাChronic itchy, inflamed dry skin patches; routine care unless secondarily infected.
    Tinea / দাদ (Fungal)Ring-shaped scaly rash; common in humid monsoon weather.
    Scabies / খোস-পাঁচড়াIntensely itchy burrows; spreads in households, requires family treatment.
    Bacterial / ব্যাকটেরিয়াজনিতCellulitis, impetigo, or infected wounds; urgent if accompanied by fever.
    Psoriasis / সোরিয়াসিসSilvery scaly plaques; requires dermatologist management.
    Healthy / সুস্থ ত্বকNo acute dermatological lesion detected.

    🌐 Phase 2 · Rural CHW & Telemedicine Network

    Community Health Workers (CHWs) capture cases offline with SkinAI Lite, sync to BD-SkinNet when signal returns, and escalate Tier 2–3 cases to the Doctor Queue and WhatsApp/Telegram bots.

    📊 Impact & Safety Policy · প্রভাব ও নীতি

    • Safety floor: Red-flag symptoms (fever, rapid spread, severe pain, bleeding) automatically enforce urgent Tier 3 referral regardless of image confidence.
    • Zero-PII default: Photos are processed in memory and discarded unless explicit anonymous 7-day clinician review consent is given.
    • Split architecture: Static frontend on Vercel + ML/RAG/PDF FastAPI backend on Hugging Face Spaces.
    ⚠️ এটি এআই স্ক্রিনিং, চিকিৎসকের বিকল্প নয় · AI screening, not a medical diagnosis.
